# MAXBE PUMPS — Premium Agriculture & Water Pump Website

## Project Objective
Design and build a highly attractive, professional, modern, fully responsive, product-focused website for **MAXBE PUMPS**, an agricultural and domestic water pump company serving farmers, households, irrigation applications, and other water-management needs.

Use the website structure and customer journey of https://www.taropumps.com/ as inspiration. Create an original MAXBE PUMPS identity with its own visual style, content, imagery, layouts, and brand personality. Do not copy Taro's logo or represent MAXBE as affiliated with Taro.

The website should look like a premium Indian pump manufacturer’s official website, designed to build trust, showcase products, help customers select pumps, and generate enquiries from farmers, homeowners, dealers, and distributors.

## 1. Brand Identity & Color Palette

Create a strong, recognizable industrial brand identity using these colors:

- **Primary red:** `#C91D28` — buttons, key highlights, product accents, important headings.
- **Premium golden yellow:** `#F4B323` — secondary buttons, highlights, icons, badges, and decorative elements.
- **Deep charcoal:** `#242424` — navigation accents, footer, dark sections, and technical information.
- **Warm off-white:** `#FAF8F2` — clean section backgrounds.
- **Pure white:** `#FFFFFF` — product cards, content areas, and forms.
- **Light agricultural green:** `#526B3D` — subtle nature-related accents only, without overpowering the red and gold branding.

Use red and gold consistently but tastefully. The website must feel premium, powerful, trustworthy, and engineered for real-world agricultural work—not cartoonish, overly colorful, or cluttered.

## 2. Overall Visual Direction

Create an impressive industrial-agriculture aesthetic with:

- Large, high-quality photographs of real water pumps and pump installations.
- Indian agricultural fields, green crops, irrigation pipes, borewells, water tanks, rural homes, and farmers using water-pumping equipment.
- Professional product photography against clean white, light grey, and warm neutral backgrounds.
- Bold typography, generous whitespace, clean grids, crisp icons, and strong visual hierarchy.
- Subtle gold lines, red call-to-action buttons, elegant shadows, and polished hover effects.
- A mix of bright, spacious product sections and dark charcoal feature sections.
- Realistic pump images with accurate proportions and visible product details.
- High-quality responsive layouts for desktop, tablet, and mobile.

Avoid generic template styling, excessive gradients, unnecessary animations, fake statistics, stock-looking illustrations, and repeated identical product images.

- Available motor ratings, voltage, phases, and discharge details when verified.
- Flow rate and head range when provided in official specifications.
- Compatible water sources and installation requirements.

- Downloadable catalogue or technical datasheet, if available.
- Installation and maintenance guidance.
- Frequently asked questions.
- Related products.

Never invent technical specifications, certifications, test results, warranty durations, or performance figures. Display only verified data and use editable placeholders for missing information.

## 9. Interactive Find Your Pump Tool

Create a user-friendly, multi-step pump finder.

Ask customers:

1. What is your main application?
   - Agriculture
   - Home water supply
   - Commercial or industrial
   - Drainage or wastewater

2. What is your water source?
   - Borewell
   - Open well
   - Water tank or sump
   - Surface water
   - Other

3. What is your approximate water requirement?

4. What is the required lifting height or total head, if known?

5. What power supply is available?
   - Single phase
   - Three phase
   - Solar, if supported
   - Not sure

6. Where will the pump be installed?

Provide a clear result showing suitable product categories based on verified product data. If important details are missing, ask the customer to contact the MAXBE team or a qualified dealer.

Include a progress indicator, clean form controls, helpful explanations, validation, and a restart option.

Do not present demo recommendations as verified engineering advice.

## 17. Design System and Interactions

Use a consistent design system throughout the website:

- Headings: bold, modern, high-impact sans-serif.
- Body: highly readable sans-serif.
- Buttons: red primary, golden-yellow secondary, with clear hover and focus states.
- Cards: clean edges, subtle shadows, consistent image ratios, and refined spacing.
- Icons: consistent, professional line icons.
- Animations: restrained fades, gentle hover elevation, and subtle transitions.
- Navigation: sticky header with functional dropdowns.
- Forms: accessible labels, useful validation, and responsive layout.

Avoid excessive rounded cards. Prefer a refined industrial style with crisp edges and strong typography.

## 18. Technical Requirements

Build a complete, multi-page, responsive website—not just a static landing page.

Requirements:
- Dedicated URLs or routes for every main page.
- Working navigation, product links, dropdowns, buttons, and forms.
- Reusable product cards and product-detail templates.
- Search and filtering for the product catalogue.
- Mobile-first responsive layouts.
- Fast-loading, optimized images.
- Accessible contrast, keyboard navigation, and semantic HTML.
- Search-engine-friendly page titles, descriptions, headings, and URLs.
- Structured product data that can be updated without redesigning the site.
- Clean loading, empty, error, and success states.
- No dead links, fake forms, or misleading demo results.
- Clearly separate verified company information from placeholder content.

Use real MAXBE PUMPS assets and official product specifications whenever supplied. If no assets are available, use high-quality, relevant pump and agriculture imagery as temporary placeholders and label them for replacement.

## Final Design Goal

The finished website should feel like a premium, established agricultural pump brand: bold red branding, elegant golden-yellow highlights, authentic Indian farming visuals, realistic product photography, clear technical information, and a smooth customer journey from discovering a pump to contacting a dealer.

Prioritize **trust, product visibility, agricultural relevance, usability, and enquiry conversion**.

Use Taro Pumps as a structural reference, but create a distinctive and original MAXBE PUMPS website rather than a direct copy of its branding or assets.
